# Key VISSIM Research Results

## Traffic Performance Improvement

The PTV VISSIM simulation results demonstrated a clear improvement in traffic operations after implementation of the Satdobato Flyover.

| Performance Measure | Improvement |
|---|---:|
| Overall Traffic Delay | **51% decrease** |
| Average Queue Length | **55% decrease** |
| Level of Service (LOS) | **Improved from E to D** |

## Model Calibration and Validation

The microscopic traffic simulation model was calibrated and validated using field-observed traffic conditions.

- **GEH statistic:** GEH values were below 5 for the evaluated traffic flows, indicating acceptable calibration.
- **NRMSE:** Values were generally below 30%, supporting acceptable model accuracy.
- The model reproduced observed traffic conditions with reasonable accuracy for evaluating the flyover's operational performance.

## Key Findings

The VISSIM analysis indicates that the Satdobato Flyover significantly improved intersection-level traffic operations.

### Main Findings

1. **51% reduction in overall traffic delay** after flyover implementation.
2. **55% reduction in average queue length**, indicating improved traffic flow.
3. **LOS improved from E to D**, representing better intersection operating conditions.
4. The calibrated and validated VISSIM model provided a reliable basis for comparing traffic conditions before and after flyover implementation.

## Research Significance

The study demonstrates how microscopic traffic simulation using **PTV VISSIM** can be applied to evaluate the operational effectiveness of urban transportation infrastructure in developing cities.

The results provide evidence that the Satdobato Flyover improved traffic efficiency and reduced congestion at the study intersection.
