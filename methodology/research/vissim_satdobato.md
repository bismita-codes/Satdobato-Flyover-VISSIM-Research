# Satdobato Flyover — VISSIM Research Methodology

## Research Title

**Analysis of the Effectiveness of the Performance of Flyover using VISSIM: A Case Study of Satdobato Intersection**

## Overview

This research evaluated the operational effectiveness of the Satdobato Flyover at the Satdobato Intersection in Lalitpur, Nepal, using **PTV VISSIM microscopic traffic simulation**.

The study compared traffic operations under existing and flyover scenarios using field-collected traffic data and simulation-based performance measures.

## Methodology

### 1. Field Data Collection

Traffic data were collected at the Satdobato Intersection to characterize existing traffic conditions.

The collected data included:

- Traffic volume
- Turning movements
- Vehicle composition
- Traffic signal information
- Queue characteristics
- Travel and delay observations

### 2. VISSIM Network Development

The Satdobato intersection and surrounding road network were modeled in **PTV VISSIM**.

The model incorporated:

- Road geometry
- Traffic lanes
- Vehicle routes
- Traffic volumes
- Vehicle composition
- Signal control
- Traffic behavior parameters

### 3. Model Calibration

The VISSIM model was calibrated using observed field conditions.

Calibration focused on ensuring that simulated traffic behavior reasonably represented real-world traffic operations.

### 4. Model Validation

The calibrated model was validated by comparing simulated and observed traffic conditions.

The study used statistical measures including:

- GEH statistic
- Normalized Root Mean Square Error (NRMSE)

Most calibration/validation measures satisfied the accepted criteria.

### 5. Scenario Analysis

Two major traffic scenarios were evaluated:

**Scenario 1 — Existing Condition**

Traffic operations under the existing intersection configuration.

**Scenario 2 — Flyover Condition**

Traffic operations after implementation of the Satdobato Flyover.

### 6. Performance Evaluation

The scenarios were compared using key traffic performance indicators:

- Average delay
- Average queue length
- Level of Service (LOS)
- Travel time
- Traffic flow performance

## Key Findings

The VISSIM simulation indicated substantial improvement in traffic operations after implementation of the flyover.

- **51% reduction in overall delay**
- **55% reduction in average queue length**
- **Level of Service improved from E to D**

These results demonstrate the usefulness of microscopic traffic simulation for evaluating intersection and flyover performance.
