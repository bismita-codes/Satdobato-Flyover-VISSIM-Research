# Research Methodology

## Study Area

The study focuses on the Satdobato Intersection and the Satdobato–Ekantakuna corridor in Lalitpur, Nepal. The intersection is an important urban transportation node experiencing substantial traffic demand and operational challenges.

## Data Collection

Field observations were conducted to obtain traffic-related data required for developing and evaluating the microscopic simulation model.

The collected data were used to represent existing traffic conditions and to evaluate the operational performance of the intersection.

## Microscopic Traffic Simulation

A microscopic traffic simulation model was developed using **PTV VISSIM**.

The model represents individual vehicle movements and interactions within the study network. The simulation was used to evaluate traffic operations and compare performance under different flyover conditions.

## Model Calibration

The simulation model was calibrated using observed field conditions to improve the representation of actual traffic behavior.

Calibration considered traffic characteristics and observed operating conditions at the study intersection.

## Model Validation

The calibrated model was validated by comparing simulated results with observed field data.

Model validation was assessed using:

- GEH statistic
- Normalized Root Mean Square Error (NRMSE)

These measures were used to evaluate the agreement between simulated and observed traffic conditions.

## Performance Measures

The effectiveness of the flyover was evaluated using key transportation performance measures, including:

- Average delay
- Queue length
- Level of Service (LOS)

The resulting performance measures were used to assess changes in traffic operations associated with the flyover.

## Research Approach

The overall research process can be summarized as:

**Field Data Collection → VISSIM Network Development → Model Calibration → Model Validation → Traffic Simulation → Performance Evaluation → Before/After Comparison**
